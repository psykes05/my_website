About Me

I am Patrick Sykes, a senior Computer Science student at Texas Tech University . My academic and professional interests are centered around software engineering, cybersecurity, information technology, artificial intelligence, and the ways modern organizations use technology to operate efficiently and securely.

Throughout my undergraduate education, I have developed experience across several areas of computer science, including programming, data structures, algorithms, software development, computer systems, cybersecurity, and web technologies. These experiences have strengthened both my technical problem-solving abilities and my understanding of how software systems support larger organizational and business objectives.

Outside of the classroom, I have been particularly interested in learning how technology functions at the enterprise level. My professional experience has allowed me to see how large organizations manage applications, infrastructure, data, cybersecurity, and IT services across complex environments. This has expanded my interests beyond simply developing software and toward understanding the full lifecycle of enterprise technology.

Following my undergraduate studies, I am pursuing a master's degree in Software and Security Engineering. Through graduate study, I plan to continue developing deeper expertise in secure software design, cybersecurity, software architecture, and the development of reliable systems. My long-term goal is to combine software engineering and cybersecurity knowledge to help organizations create technology that is both useful and secure.

Professional Experience

IT Service Management Intern

Tenet Healthcare

During my internship with Tenet Healthcare, I gained experience working within a large enterprise IT environment. My work focused primarily on IT Service Management, application infrastructure, ServiceNow, and improving the quality and organization of enterprise technology data.

  • Worked with ServiceNow and its Configuration Management Database (CMDB) to better understand relationships between business applications and the infrastructure supporting them.
  • Collaborated with application owners to gather information about application environments, servers, domains, integrations, ownership, and technical dependencies.
  • Helped map applications to their underlying infrastructure to provide greater visibility into enterprise systems and application dependencies.
  • Contributed to CMDB cleanup and data-quality initiatives, including identifying duplicate application records, confirming application ownership, and improving the accuracy of application information.
  • Developed a standardized Quick Reference Guide that documented the process for gathering application information and entering it into ServiceNow, helping create a repeatable workflow that could be used across the organization.
  • Explored how accurate application and infrastructure data can support future artificial intelligence and automation capabilities within IT operations.

My experience at Tenet gave me the opportunity to apply concepts from computer science in a real-world enterprise setting while also developing professional communication skills. Working with application owners and IT professionals showed me the importance of collaboration between technical teams and business stakeholders.

The internship also increased my interest in the intersection of software, infrastructure, cybersecurity, and enterprise technology. Understanding how applications depend on servers, databases, networks, and other systems reinforced the importance of maintaining accurate technical information, particularly when organizations are managing thousands of interconnected technology assets.
